## Workshop 4: Integrated Photonics, From LiDAR Chip to CPO/LPO

Prof. Younghyun Kim spoke in Session 2 (CPO/LPO) of Workshop 4, then joined the session's panel discussion. In the talk he presented SiN-on-Glass (SiNG) as a photonic packaging platform for next-generation co-packaged optics (CPO) and described a set of passive and active components built on glass for dense optical interconnects. On the panel, he discussed the outlook and open challenges for CPO and linear-drive pluggable optics (LPO) with researchers from industry and academia.
