## Highlight

ASDL members attended ISMP 2026, the 24th International Symposium on Microelectronics Packaging, held at Paradise Hotel Busan, Korea, on November 3-6, 2026.

Organized by the Korean Microelectronics Packaging Society (KMEPS), ISMP is an international forum for advances in electronic packaging materials, processes, equipment, design, and reliability. The 2026 program covered topics including chiplet package design, 2.xD/3.xD integration, hybrid bonding, co-packaged optics (CPO), and thermal management.

Seokyoung Shin, Baehun Jo, and Woojae Kim presented three posters during the November 5 poster session, covering optical coupling, adhesive-bonded interfaces, and microfluidic cooling for CPO. These contributions addressed optical interconnection, high-power reliability, and thermal management within the conference's broader packaging scope.

- **P1-16 | Seokyoung Shin:** Design and Fabrication of an Evanescent Coupler for Optical Coupling in Co-Packaged Optics
- **P1-17 | Baehun Jo:** High-Power Reliability Study of Adhesive-Bonded Chip-to-Fiber Interfaces for Co-Packaged Optics
- **P1-19 | Woojae Kim:** Thermal Test Vehicle for Co-Packaged Optics: PDMS Microfluidic Cooling Integrated with Glass-Based SiN Microring Resonators

The program excerpts below highlight the three contributions and their presenters. Source: ISMP 2026 Program Book v7, PDF pages 48-49 (printed pages 36-37).
