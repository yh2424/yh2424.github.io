Devices

  • Optical RDLSiN waveguides on glass: low loss, high density, multilayer routing and high reliability across the panel.
  • PICSi photonic chips with high-speed optical modulators (MRM, MZM) next to each ASIC.
  • LD · SOA · SwitchLight sources, SOA integration and non-volatile optical switches for the optical network on glass.

Packaging

  • Fiber-to-GlassOptical coupling from fiber into the glass panel with grating couplers (GC) and edge couplers (EC).
  • Glass-to-PICEvanescent couplers (EVC) between SiN-on-Glass and the Si photonic chip.
